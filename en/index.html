Kyoto Goshuin: Rakuyo 33 — Support

Provider: Dr. Hac

Kyoto Goshuin: Rakuyo 33 (洛陽三十三所 御朱印めぐり帳) is an iPhone / iPad app for recording the goshuin (the calligraphy and seals received at a temple), travel photos and journal entries from your pilgrimage on the Rakuyō 33 Kannon Pilgrimage (33 Kannon temples in the city of Kyoto). It is available in Japanese, English, Spanish, Traditional Chinese and Korean. The App does not grant goshuin; it is a record book for the goshuin you receive at the temples.

FAQ

Q. Where are my records stored?
Only on your device and in your own iCloud (if you have purchased the full version or are in a trial). They are not sent to the provider’s server.

Q. Can I keep my records when I change devices?
If iCloud sync is on with the same Apple ID, they carry over automatically. You can also save a copy on hand via “Settings > Export records to this device (ZIP)”.

Q. Can I use my “Unlock all 33 temples” purchase on another device?
You can restore it via “Settings > Restore purchase” (the same Apple ID is required). Family Sharing is also supported.

Q. Where do the temple photos come from?
The photos are Creative Commons or public-domain images from Wikimedia Commons. Attributions are listed under “Settings > Photo credits”.

Q. Visiting hours or goshuin hours differ from the actual ones.
They are based on official information but may change. Please let us know at the contact above. When visiting, please follow each temple’s guidance.
